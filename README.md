# AI Notebook

A personal knowledge base for exploring artificial intelligence, large language models, and their practical applications.

## Topics

- **Instructions:** Reusable LLM instructions and prompt experiments.
- **Concepts:** Explanations and reference notes about AI technologies.
- **Experiments:** Hands-on tests, model comparisons, and technical observations.
- **Articles:** Reading notes and commentary on external sources.
- **Thoughts:** Personal reflections, hypotheses, and open questions.
- **Workflows:** Practical techniques, tools, and repeatable processes.

## Approach

This repository combines external research with personal observations and experiments. Notes should distinguish sourced claims from personal interpretations, document meaningful uncertainty, and capture lessons that may be useful later.

The contents are a working knowledge base, not necessarily polished or definitive conclusions.

## Getting Started

1. Browse [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) for an overview of the notes.
2. Use the templates in [`templates/`](templates/) when adding article notes or experiments.
3. Add new material in the most relevant topic folder. Keep the structure lightweight and evolve it as needed.
